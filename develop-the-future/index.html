{develop the future}

Helping students turn an idea into an app, and seeing themselves in tech industry.

Develop the Future brings code, creativity, and industry mentorship together. Through the Career Education Council, I support students as they shape ideas, learn app-development foundations, and build the confidence to share what they create.

Develop the Future × AppleFrom sketch to SwiftUI

My role

Guidance that meets students where they are.

As a Program Facilitator and Technical Mentor, I help make the path from first concept to working prototype feel approachable. That means explaining technical ideas clearly, working through roadblocks, and keeping each student's own perspective at the centre of the project.

The goal is bigger than learning a tool: students practise creative problem-solving, collaboration, pitching, and the confidence to imagine themselves in a technology career.
